# Use the restored account sections

The [illustrated role guide](../../system-walkthrough/09-restored-role-pages/README.md) shows every restored section with synthetic records. Production records are never filled with those fixtures.

1. Open **Dashboard**. Use your role's **Quick actions**. Instructors see their saved syllabus lifecycle and draft review queue; students can **Continue learning**. Dean/Associate Dean review course readiness and faculty coverage.
2. In **Performance**, choose **Course**, and where available **Block**, **From** and **Through**. **Overview** shows current assessment radar, saved-attempt history and the scoring matrix. Select a learner for the detail dialog. **By Topic** discloses unavailable topic mapping and shows assessment coverage instead of guessed mastery. **Learning Materials** and **Assessment Scores** provide record tables and **Download CSV**. **Alerts** has a local score-review threshold.
3. Instructors can open **Student Monitoring** or the `/scoring-sheet` bookmark. Switch between **Assessment Scores** and **Learning Material Access**. An absent attempt is distinct from zero; an open does not prove reading completion. Submission rate compares current-version attempts with the confirmed roster.
4. Dean/Associate Dean open **Monitor** and switch among **Syllabus Status**, **Delivery Progress**, **Student Oversight** and **Alerts**. Expand a course in **Course Loading → Course Monitoring** for publication and roster details. **Instructors** expands account-level delivery summaries; unavailable qualifications are disclosed. Associate Dean cannot open Performance or inspect scores.
5. In **Records**, use **Blocks / Sections** to select a block, or **Courses** to review the authorized projections. In **File Import**, select **Course Records**, **Course Loads** or **Student Class Lists**. Download the matching header file under **Templates**. Choose a CSV up to 1 MB, inspect valid/rejected rows, then press **Confirm Import**. Only valid rows are saved to your private account intake. Refresh and inspect **History** to verify the save. Import errors or revision conflicts preserve the source preview; a success message is shown only after persistence.
6. Students open **My Courses**, filter by course/title, expand a week and choose **Open material**. Switch between **Document** and **Presentation**; the document's **Download PDF** exports that lesson. In **Assessments**, choose **Open assessment**, use numbered question buttons, answer every item, then **Confirm and submit responses**. A saved result remains read-only. The server supports one objective attempt per published version; the elapsed timer does not impose a deadline.
7. Open **Notifications** to review current record notices. **Mark All Read** is saved for that account/role on this device. New/unopened records remain distinct from an email delivery service. The top-bar theme control or Appearance settings switches to solid dark surfaces.

**Refresh records** reloads the shared authorized projection. **Export authorized records** downloads it as JSON. **Export backup** downloads the separate owner workspace, including private intake. The institution's official loading, approvals and grade computations remain in their established systems.
