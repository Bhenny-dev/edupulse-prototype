# Compact AI providers and settings simplification

11 November 2026. Local implementation; no deployment performed.

Settings → **AI & Knowledge** now shows **ChatGPT plan**, **OpenAI API**, **Claude**, **Gemini** and **OpenRouter** as compact logo/name/**Connect** cards. Gemini selects the already-supported account-bound API-key and model flow. OpenRouter retains its browser authorization. ChatGPT plan connection remains unavailable without approval; subscription and approval explanations are under **Connection details**.

The owner also removed the **Knowledge library** settings panel, including its upload/paste area, document table and comparison controls. Obsolete capture scenes and screenshots are removed. Existing account documents, private retrieval, Pulse attachments and syllabus reference intake remain; the former Pulse link to the removed panel is gone. This change does not delete business data or change authorization, storage, provider billing or schema.

Provider marks are bundled local SVGs under the included MIT attribution, with no new dependency or runtime third-party image request. Cards wrap on phone screens; monochrome marks adapt to dark mode. The [provider manual](../../system-manual/08-ai-connections/README.md), [private source guidance](../../system-manual/04-knowledge-library/README.md), role walkthroughs and capture pipeline match the simplified interface. See [validation](validation/results.md).
